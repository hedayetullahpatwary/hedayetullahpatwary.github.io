👤 Who I Am

About Me

I am a Computer Science & Engineering graduate from AIUB, majoring in Information Systems. My research focuses on Explainable AI, dual-backbone hybrid vision architectures and evidence-constrained LLM applications: models that are accurate and can also show why they reached a decision.

As a Research Assistant I led the architecture of OliveLeafNet (99.07% test accuracy) and developed the Constrained Evidence Reasoning framework presented at IEEE QPAIN 2026. On the engineering side I build C# / .NET, Java and PHP applications on well-normalized SQL databases, following Clean Architecture, MVC, automated testing and Scrum.
